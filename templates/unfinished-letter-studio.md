# The Unfinished Letter Studio

*Finish the letter you have been meaning to write*

**Category:** Grown-ups · **Ages:** Ages 18+ · **Chinese name:** 未完成信件工作室 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

Everyone carries one: the thank-you never sent, the apology that stalled at the second sentence, the congratulations that stayed a draft. This studio finishes that letter with you, one honest step at a time. You choose the kind of letter and the tone, recall one specific real detail — a Tuesday, a borrowed ladder, a sentence someone actually said — and Muse helps you shape it into a letter that sounds like you on your clearest day, never like a greeting card. Gentle prompts arrive one at a time so the page never feels like an exam. You can keep the finished letter as a private draft, prepare a clean print-ready copy, or practise reading it aloud first. No templates-with-blanks, no stock phrases, no sending from inside the app — the letter stays yours until you decide what to do with it.

## Features

- One specific memory detail does the heavy lifting — prompts help you find it
- Gentle prompts one at a time, a full outline first, or draft-then-revise — your choice
- Tone you control: warm and simple, formal, playful, or quiet and heartfelt
- Finish your way: private draft, print-ready copy, or read-aloud practice
- Nothing is ever sent from the app — you decide if and how the letter travels

## What's included

- Guided letter session (find the detail, shape, revise)
- Six letter kinds with tone control
- Print-ready copy formatter
- Read-aloud practice mode

## At a glance

- The detail prompt — 'What is one specific thing they did that you can still picture? A day, a place, a sentence?'
- Draft view — your letter shaped around that one detail, in the tone you chose, with no stock phrases.
- Finish screen — keep it private, make a print-ready copy, or practise reading it aloud before it travels.

## How it works

1. Choose — pick the letter kind, tone, and length.
2. Remember — answer one prompt to find the one specific detail the letter turns on.
3. Shape — Muse helps you turn the detail into a full draft in your own voice.
4. Finish — revise once, then keep it private, print it, or practise reading it aloud.

## Sample content

### Sample Session · A Thank-You Letter to a Neighbour (complete worked session)

SETUP: Thank-you letter, Warm & simple tone, Standard letter, Gentle prompts one at a time. PROMPT 1 — THE DETAIL: Muse asks: 'What is one specific thing Sam did that you can still picture — a day, a place, a sentence?' ALEX ANSWERS: 'The Saturday the fence blew down in the storm. Sam was at the gate by eight with a drill, two coffees, and his father's old level. He never once said it was a bother.' PROMPT 2 — WHAT IT MEANT: Muse asks: 'What did that morning change for you?' ALEX ANSWERS: 'I had just moved in and still felt like a guest on my own street. After that day I stopped feeling like one.' THE FINISHED LETTER (as the studio shapes it): 'Dear Sam, I have started this letter three times since February, which tells you how much the fence mattered and how bad I am at saying so. On the Saturday after the storm you were at my gate by eight, drill in one hand, two coffees in the other, and your father's level under your arm. You stayed until the last panel was true. You never once called it a favour, so I never got to call it one back — until now. I moved here feeling like a guest on my own street. Somewhere between the second coffee and the last screw, that stopped being true. Thank you — for the fence, and for the street. — Alex' REVISION NOTE (sample): Muse suggests cutting one stock phrase ('words cannot express') because the letter already shows, in one pictured morning, exactly what words would only claim. Alex agrees. The letter is kept as a private draft.
