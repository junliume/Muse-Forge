# The Place Value Post Office

*Sort parcels by hundreds, tens and ones*

**Category:** Education · **Ages:** Ages 6–9 · **Chinese name:** 数位邮局 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

A hands-on place-value studio set in a friendly post office: every number arrives as a parcel that must be sorted into hundreds, tens and ones bays before it can be delivered. Learners build numbers with place blocks, write expanded form, compare two parcels by looking at hundreds first, then tens, then ones, and round parcels to the nearest ten or hundred for the fast-delivery shelf. Difficulty grows only when sorting is steady.

## Sample content

### Sample Lesson · Sorting Day at the Post Office: 247, 83 and 305 (complete worked lesson)

WARM-UP: Count by tens: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50. What is 10 more than 63? [73]

SORTER 1 — Parcel 247: 2 hundreds, 4 tens, 7 ones. Expanded form: 200 + 40 + 7 = 247.

SORTER 2 — Parcel 83: 0 hundreds, 8 tens, 3 ones. Expanded form: 80 + 3 = 83. Note: a two-digit parcel leaves the hundreds bay empty — that is fine, the bay is simply waiting.

SORTER 3 — Parcel 305: 3 hundreds, 0 tens, 5 ones. Expanded form: 300 + 0 + 5 = 305. Note: the zero holds the tens place — without it, 305 would collapse to 35, a completely different parcel.

COMPARE — 247 vs 274: look at hundreds first — they are equal (2 and 2), so move to tens: 4 vs 7, so 274 is larger. The ones are not looked at until hundreds and tens tie.

ROUNDING — for the fast-delivery shelf: 247 to the nearest ten — the ones digit is 7, which rounds up, so 250. 83 to the nearest ten is 80. 305 to the nearest hundred is 300.

TRY IT (answers in brackets): Build 164 [1 hundred, 6 tens, 4 ones; 100 + 60 + 4]. Which is larger, 319 or 291? [319 — 319 has 3 hundreds and 291 has 2 hundreds, so 319 is larger, decided on hundreds alone]. Round 164 to the nearest ten [160].

CLOSE: the learner re-sorts 247 from memory and says its expanded form aloud: 200 + 40 + 7.
