# Curious Minds: Science Lab

*Ask, predict, test, and explain — real science thinking for young kids.*

**Category:** Education · **Ages:** Ages 5–8 · **Chinese name:** 好奇心科学实验室 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

Every lab runs the real scientific loop: Observe → Wonder → Predict (locked in, with a “because” reason, before any test) → Test → Explain with a claim and its evidence. Virtual mode is the default; a parent-confirmed at-home mode uses household-safe materials only, behind a safety gate that stays locked on. Predictions can never be quietly edited after the test — changing your mind out loud, with evidence, is the celebrated move.

## Sample content

### Lab 1 · Sink or Float Detective (complete)

Observe: a wooden block, a metal spoon, a leaf, an orange. Wonder: “What makes things float?” Predict (locked): “The orange will sink BECAUSE it is heavy.” Test: the block floats, the spoon sinks, the leaf floats — and the orange floats! Explain (claim + evidence): “My claim changed: heaviness alone doesn't decide. Evidence: the heavy orange floated and the lighter spoon sank.” The surprise re-test: peel the orange and test again — the peeled orange sinks. New claim: “The peel traps air, and the air helps it float. Evidence: same orange, no peel, sank.” That is the whole method in one lab: predict honestly, test fairly, and let the evidence change your mind.
