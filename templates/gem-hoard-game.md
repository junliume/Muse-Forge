# The Gem Hoard Game

*A take-turns gem game where counting is the strategy*

**Category:** Games · **Ages:** Ages 6–10 · **Chinese name:** 宝石宝库大挑战 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

A take-turns strategy game built on one beautiful secret: leave a multiple of 4. Two players (or a player against Muse) take 1, 2 or 3 gems from a sleeping dragon's hoard; whoever takes the last gem wins the round. Every game is counted out loud together, every worked game is checked gem by gem, and the winning pattern is discovered, not told — players meet the 'cold pile' of 4 first, then watch the multiples of 4 march down the hoard. Includes a misère variant where the last gem wakes the dragon. No racing, no elimination between rounds, and a helper that hints at the pattern instead of playing your turn.

## Features

- One secret, discovered not told — leave a multiple of 4
- Fully worked first game, checked gem by gem (10 + 11 = 21)
- Misère variant: Don't Wake the Dragon flips the ending
- Helper hints at the pattern — never plays your turn

## What's included

- Hoard game board & gem counter
- Worked first game with verified strategy
- Misère variant: Don't Wake the Dragon
- Rematch hoard sizes (15 / 21 / 25)

## At a glance

- 21 gems, two players, one secret — whoever takes the last gem wins the round
- The cold pile: with 4 gems, every first move loses — the whole strategy hides in that one fact
- Don't Wake the Dragon: the misère variant where the last gem loses and 17-13-9-5-1 wins

## How it works

1. Set the hoard (15, 21 or 25 gems) and read the rules aloud together
2. Take turns taking 1, 2 or 3 gems, counting the pile out loud after every turn
3. Spot the multiples-of-4 pattern, then flip the game with Don't Wake the Dragon

## Sample content

### The First Game · 21 Gems, Fully Worked (with the multiples-of-4 secret)

THE GEM HOARD GAME — a complete first game (about 25 minutes)

YOU NEED: 21 gems in a pile in the middle (buttons, pebbles or counters all work — they are the sleeping dragon's hoard, and the dragon is only pretend). Two players sit on opposite sides. Decide who plays first — the first game below is fully worked out, so read it together before you play your own.

THE RULES (read aloud together): 1) On your turn, take 1, 2 or 3 gems from the hoard — never 0, never 4 or more. 2) The player who takes the LAST gem wins the round and gets to hide the hoard for the next round. 3) Count the pile out loud together after every turn — the count is the whole game. 4) No take-backs: once your hand leaves the gems, the turn is done. If a plan feels wrong, the helper hints — it never plays your turn for you.

WARM-UP (2 minutes): With 4 gems, can the first player win? Try all three moves: take 1 leaves 3, take 2 leaves 2, take 3 leaves 1 — and in every case the second player takes ALL the rest and wins. So 4 is a 'cold pile': whoever has to move on 4 loses against perfect play. Remember the cold pile. It is the key to the whole game.

THE WORKED GAME — Alex plays first against Sam. Hoard: 21 gems.
TURN 1 — Alex takes 1. Hoard: 20. (Alex whispers the plan: 'Land on a multiple of 4.')
TURN 2 — Sam takes 3. Hoard: 17.
TURN 3 — Alex takes 1 (3 + 1 = 4). Hoard: 16.
TURN 4 — Sam takes 2. Hoard: 14.
TURN 5 — Alex takes 2 (2 + 2 = 4). Hoard: 12.
TURN 6 — Sam takes 1. Hoard: 11.
TURN 7 — Alex takes 3 (1 + 3 = 4). Hoard: 8.
TURN 8 — Sam takes 3. Hoard: 5.
TURN 9 — Alex takes 1 (3 + 1 = 4). Hoard: 4. (The cold pile — and it is Sam's turn.)
TURN 10 — Sam takes 2. Hoard: 2.
TURN 11 — Alex takes 2 — the last gems. Hoard: 0. Alex wins the round.
Check the totals together: Alex took 1+1+2+3+1+2 = 10 gems; Sam took 3+2+1+3+2 = 11 gems; 10 + 11 = 21. Sam took MORE gems and still lost — because winning is about the LAST gem, not the most gems.

THE SECRET, SPOTTED: After every Alex turn, the hoard was 20, 16, 12, 8, 4, 0 — the multiples of 4. Sam could only ever take 1, 2 or 3, so Alex answered with 4 minus Sam's number and always landed back on a multiple of 4. The strategy in one line: LEAVE A MULTIPLE OF 4. It works from 21 because 21 is not a multiple of 4 — the first player takes 1 and takes control. DISCOVERY CHALLENGE: start a round on exactly 20 gems. Now the SECOND player can use the same secret. Try it and see who wins.

VARIANT — DON'T WAKE THE DRAGON (misère): same turns, but whoever takes the last gem LOSES (they woke the dragon). The secret changes: leave 17, 13, 9, 5, then 1 — the numbers that are 1 more than a multiple of 4. From 21, the second player can mirror: whatever the first player takes, answer to land on 17, 13, 9, 5, and finally leave exactly 1 gem, which the first player must take. Try the worked game again under dragon rules and watch the ending flip.

REMATCH IDEAS: quick hoard of 15 (first player: take 3 to land on 12); long hoard of 25 (take 1 to land on 24); play cooperatively against the hoard — can the team force a win against the mirror strategy? (It cannot, from a multiple of 4 — prove it together.)
