# The Number Line Ferry

*Ride the ferry, hop the number line, land exactly on target*

**Category:** Education · **Ages:** Ages 6–9 · **Chinese name:** 数轴小渡船 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

A gentle number-line maths studio where learners ride a little ferry between numbered docks. Each crossing is a hop: count the jump, find the missing dock, and check by hopping back. Learners build fluency with addition, subtraction and missing numbers from 0 to 100 — always by moving, counting and verifying, never by guessing.

## Features

- Hop-and-check crossings — every answer is verified by hopping back
- Missing-dock puzzles — find the start, the jump, or the landing
- Two rulers, one idea — 0–20 harbour and 0–100 river use the same hop language
- Parent view — hops attempted, reverses checked, and steady skills at a glance

## What's included

- Number-line ferry board (0–20 and 0–100)
- Worked crossing library
- Missing-dock puzzle sets
- Parent hop log

## At a glance

- A ferry leaves Dock 7, hops +8, and lands on Dock 15 — count each pier as you pass
- Missing dock: the ferry lands on 42 after a +17 hop — hop back 17 to find Dock 25
- River crossing to 100: 36 + 24 by a +20 bridge hop then +4, landing exactly on 60

## Sample content

### Sample Crossing · Harbour Route: The +8 Ferry to Dock 15 (fully worked)

SET-UP: Harbour line 0–20, ferry starts at Dock 7.

CROSSING 1: hop +8. Count on: 7 → 8 (1), 9 (2), 10 (3), 11 (4), 12 (5), 13 (6), 14 (7), 15 (8). Landing: Dock 15, so 7 + 8 = 15.

CHECK BY HOPPING BACK: 15 − 8: 15 → 14 (1), 13 (2), 12 (3), 11 (4), 10 (5), 9 (6), 8 (7), 7 (8) — back at the start, correct.

CROSSING 2 (missing jump): start 9, land 17. Count the piers passed: 10 (1), 11 (2), 12 (3), 13 (4), 14 (5), 15 (6), 16 (7), 17 (8) — jump is +8, so 9 + 8 = 17.

CROSSING 3 (missing start, river line): land 42 after +17. Hop back: 42 − 10 = 32, 32 − 7 = 25, so start was Dock 25; check forward 25 + 17 = 42 (25 + 10 = 35, 35 + 7 = 42).

BRIDGE HOP: 36 + 24 = hop +20 to 56, then +4 to 60.

TRY IT (with answers): a) 6 + 9 = 15, b) 13 − 5 = 8, c) start ? + 12 = 31, answer 19 (31 − 12 = 19).
