# Word Lab: Vocabulary & Reading Quest

*Learn powerful words in real stories — then use them like a writer.*

**Category:** Education · **Also listed under:** Storytelling · **Ages:** Ages 6–10 · **Chinese name:** 词汇实验室 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

Tier 2 vocabulary learned the way strong readers actually learn it: each word is met at least 3 times inside real story contexts, predicted from clues before any definition appears, then produced in the child's own sentence by hold-to-talk. Every chapter ends with a recorded retell for parents, and review follows a D+1/3/7 spaced schedule. No speed rewards, no rankings — the win is using the word like a writer.

## Sample content

### Chapter 1 · The Lighthouse Picnic (enormous / whisper / discover)

The story opens: “Mina carried the enormous picnic basket down the cliff path. It was bigger than her little brother. It was bigger than the dog.” Guided exercise 1 — Predict from clues: “The basket is enormous. Which clue tells you enormous means very big?” (It is compared to the brother and the dog.) Exercise 2 — Word in the wild: “Grandpa had to whisper, because the baby gulls were asleep” — what happens to a voice when someone whispers? Exercise 3 — Notice the pattern: in “Under the loose stone, Mina would discover…”, the story pauses — what do you predict she discovers, and which words made you think so? Exercise 4 — Say it yourself (hold-to-talk): use discover in a sentence about your own kitchen. Exercise 5 — Retell: tell the chapter back in your own words, using at least two of this chapter's words; the recording goes to your parent. Method taught: meet the word → predict from clues → check in a new context → say it yourself → meet it again on D+1, D+3, D+7.
