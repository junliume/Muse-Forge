# The Slow Stitch Sampler

*Stitch a sampler one quiet row at a time*

**Category:** Grown-ups · **Ages:** Adults · **Chinese name:** 慢针线刺绣样册 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

A calm hand-embroidery studio for grown-ups: choose a sampler theme and thread palette, and the studio teaches each stitch with slow, exact steps — how to start, where the needle goes, how many stitches a row takes — then walks you row by row until a small finished sampler is ready to frame or gift. Sessions are unhurried and private, tension is taught as a feel (snug, never puckered), and every sample row in the studio is fully worked with real stitch counts. No kits to buy inside the app, no streaks, no speed scores — just cloth, thread, and one quiet row at a time.

## Features

- Each stitch taught with exact needle positions and row counts

## Sample content

### Sample Session · Sampler One: Four Starter Rows, Worked Stitch by Stitch (complete, counts verified)

SETUP (2 minutes): Hoop a square of natural linen so it sounds like a soft drum when tapped. Thread a needle with two strands of plum thread and knot the end once. The studio marks one straight practice line, 100 mm long, for each row. Work every row left to right.

ROW 1 — RUNNING STITCH (the dashed line): Bring the needle up at the start of the line, down 5 mm later, up 5 mm after that, down 5 mm after that — in, out, in, out, like a dolphin. Each repeat uses 10 mm of line (5 mm of stitch on top, 5 mm of space underneath), so the 100 mm line holds exactly 10 visible dashes. Check: count your dashes — if you have 10 and they look even, Row 1 is done.

ROW 2 — BACKSTITCH (the solid line): Bring the needle up at the start, go down 5 mm BEHIND the start (into the end of the line's margin), then come up 5 mm AHEAD of your start and go back down into the hole you just left. Every stitch travels backward to meet the last one, so no gaps can appear. At 5 mm per stitch, the 100 mm line takes exactly 20 stitches. Check: turn the cloth over — the back shows stitches twice as long, which is how you know each stitch landed in the previous hole.

ROW 3 — CROSS STITCH (the row of X's): Work 8 crosses. For each cross, stitch one diagonal (bottom-left to top-right), then the second diagonal over it (bottom-right to top-left). That is 2 diagonal stitches per cross, so 8 crosses take exactly 16 diagonal stitches. Golden rule: every top diagonal must lean the same way — here, all top stitches lean from bottom-right to top-left — or the row looks unsettled. Check: run a fingertip along the row; it should feel like a tiny plait.

ROW 4 — FRENCH KNOTS (the dotted row): Bring the needle up, wrap the thread around the needle twice, hold the wraps snug, and push the needle back down one thread-width away from where it rose — never in the same hole, or the knot pulls through. Make 5 knots, spaced evenly. Five knots, two wraps each. Check: each knot should sit ON the cloth like a small bead, not sink into it.

SIGN THE CORNER: In the bottom-right corner, backstitch your initials using the Row 2 method at 3 mm per stitch — small, neat, yours.

TENSION NOTE (read once, use forever): thread should lie snug against the cloth. If the cloth puckers, the stitch was pulled too hard; slide the needle under the last three stitches, ease a whisper of thread back, and continue more gently. A sampler is not a test — a slightly uneven row is a record of a real hand, and the studio never asks you to unpick a whole row for one stitch.
