# The Marigold Door Tales

*A tiny garden door to a new co-created world each evening*

**Category:** Storytelling · **Ages:** Ages 4–9 · **Chinese name:** 万寿菊小门故事 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

A gentle take-turns storytelling template. Each evening Alex finds a small marigold door at the bottom of the garden — a door that was not there yesterday. Behind it waits one tiny world chosen together: Umbrella Hill, the Teacup Sea, the Leaf Library, the Clock Garden, or the Picnic Moon. Muse offers one slow line, the child continues the story, Muse repeats the child's line back to confirm, then offers the next line. The child always leads the plot; companions only add warmth and gentle comedy, never clues, answers, or spoilers. Sessions end with a cozy close, a cliffhanger for tomorrow, or a short poem close, and parents get a simple story log of what was co-created.

## Sample content

### Sample · Evening One: Alex and the Umbrella Hill (complete co-created opening)

MUSE (one slow line to begin): 'On the last evening of spring, Alex found a small door in the marigold bed — a door painted red, no taller than a teacup, that had not been there yesterday.' ALEX'S LINE: 'Alex knocked twice, very politely, because small doors like to be asked.' MUSE (repeating Alex's line back to confirm, then one new line): 'Alex knocked twice, very politely, because small doors like to be asked. The door opened with a tiny creak, and warm wind smelled of rain and honey.' THE CHOICE: Behind the door lay Umbrella Hill, where every hill wears an umbrella hat. Milo the mole gardener tipped his leaf cap. 'Mind the puddles,' he said. 'They remember every splash.' ALEX'S LINE: 'Alex stepped in a puddle on purpose, just a little, to see what it remembered.' MUSE (confirming, then continuing): 'Alex stepped in a puddle on purpose, just a little, and the puddle showed a silver fish, a lost button, and last Tuesday's rainbow.' Bram the hedgehog rolled down the hill inside his umbrella, laughing. 'Slower!' called Lulu the firefly, glowing twice for yes. THE COZY CLOSE (chosen ending): Alex promised the hill, 'I will come back tomorrow and bring a story of my own.' Milo planted that promise beside the marigolds, where good promises grow. And the small red door closed — softly, so as not to wake the seeds.
