# Rhythm Relay Lab

*Listen, remember, and play the beat back together*

**Category:** Games · **Ages:** Ages 6–10 · **Chinese name:** 节奏接力实验室 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

A listen-and-repeat rhythm memory game that grows one beat at a time. Hear a short pattern, see it as friendly beat cards, then play it back with claps, a desk drum, or body percussion. Each round replays the exact same pattern plus one new beat, so memory builds by listening, not by speed. There are no timers, no speed scores, and no failing out: miss a beat and the pattern simply replays more slowly, as many times as needed. Play solo, in a partner relay where each player adds a beat and passes it on, or in a group circle.

## Features

- Patterns grow one beat at a time — listen, watch the beat cards, play it back
- Partner relay and group circle modes for playing together
- No timers, no speed scores, unlimited slow replays
- Visual beat cards for every sound, so you can see the rhythm too
- Grown-up session summary: patterns completed and longest chain

## Sample content

### Sample · Level 3: The Garden Gate Beat (complete listen-and-repeat level)

LEVEL 3 — THE GARDEN GATE BEAT. Sounds in this level: CLAP (hands together), TAP (desk drum, one soft hit), REST (hands still, count silently). Tempo: slow and steady, one beat per second. Beat cards show a yellow circle for CLAP, a blue square for TAP, and an empty outline for REST.

ROUND 1 — 3 beats: CLAP · CLAP · REST. Host line: 'Listen first. Clap, clap, rest — the gate swings open, then it waits.' The player watches the three cards light up in time, then plays it back. Check: beat 1 CLAP, beat 2 CLAP, beat 3 REST (hands still for a full count). If beat 3 is clapped by mistake, the host says: 'So close — the third beat is the quiet one. Listen once more, slowly,' and replays at half speed. No score changes.

ROUND 2 — 4 beats: CLAP · CLAP · REST · TAP. Host line: 'Same gate, one new sound at the very end: a soft tap, like knocking once before you go in.' Verify before playing: rounds so far are CLAP-CLAP-REST, then CLAP-CLAP-REST-TAP — the first three beats did not change, only beat 4 is new. The player plays all four beats back.

ROUND 3 — 5 beats: CLAP · CLAP · REST · TAP · CLAP. Host line: 'One more clap to close the gate behind you.' Full check, beat by beat: 1 CLAP, 2 CLAP, 3 REST, 4 TAP, 5 CLAP. The player plays the full five-beat chain. Two slow replays are always offered before any try.

PARTNER RELAY FINISH (optional, 2 minutes): Player One plays beats 1–3, Player Two plays beats 4–5, then they swap halves and play again, so both players practise both halves. LEVEL COMPLETE when the full 5-beat pattern is played back correctly once in solo mode or once with swapped halves in relay mode. Grown-up summary line for this level: 'Longest chain: 5 beats · Sounds used: clap, tap, rest · Replays used: however many were needed — replays are always free.'
