# Weekend Reset Studio

*A calm 25-minute reset for a home that breathes again*

**Category:** Grown-ups · **Ages:** Grown-ups · **Chinese name:** 周末焕新工作室 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

A gentle, timer-free home reset planner for grown-ups. Pick a room, a time box and an energy level, and the app builds a short, ordered reset session — clear, clean, put back, and one small delight at the end. No streak shaming, no scores, no before/after photos required. Sessions end with a two-line reflection you can keep in a Reset Journal. Designed for real homes and tired evenings: every session can be paused, shortened, or split across two days.

## Features

- Room-by-room reset sessions in 15, 25 or 40 minutes
- Energy-aware ordering: lightest task first when energy is low
- Pause, shorten or split any session — no streaks, no scores
- Reset Journal: a two-line reflection after each session
- One small delight step at the end of every session

## Sample content

### Sample Session · Living Room, 25 Minutes, Low Energy (complete session)

BEFORE YOU START (1 minute): Put on music you like. Carry a laundry basket, a small bin bag, and a cloth to the living room door. That basket is your only tool — everything that belongs elsewhere goes in it, nothing gets put away mid-session yet.

STEP 1 — CLEAR THE SEATING (5 minutes, lightest first): Shake crumbs from cushions onto the floor, straighten cushions, fold the throw over the arm of the sofa. If a cushion cover is stained, set it by the door for laundry — do not start laundry now.

STEP 2 — SURFACES ONLY (6 minutes): Work left to right around the room. Coffee table: stack books into one pile, coasters in one place, remotes in a row. Shelf tops: only move what is visibly dusty or out of place. Everything that lives in another room goes into the basket.

STEP 3 — FLOOR PASS (5 minutes): Pick up everything on the floor into the basket or bin. Quick sweep or vacuum of the centre rug only — edges can wait for a deep-clean day. Empty the small bin if it is more than half full.

STEP 4 — PUT THE BASKET AWAY (5 minutes): Walk the basket room to room and deliver each item to its home. If an item has no home, it goes on the kitchen counter as a single "needs a home" pile — decide its home tomorrow, not tonight.

STEP 5 — ONE SMALL DELIGHT (2 minutes): Choose one: water the plant, light a candle, open the window for fresh air, or put fresh water and a small snack on the side table for your evening sit-down.

PAUSE AND REFLECT (1 minute): Sit in the reset room for ten slow breaths. Write two lines in your Reset Journal: 1) What feels calmer now? 2) What is the one thing you will not do tonight? Example entry: "The sofa looks like an invitation again. Tonight I will not sort the bookcase — it can wait for Saturday."

IF YOU RUN OUT OF ENERGY EARLY: Stop after any step, put the basket by the door, and mark the session "split" — the app resumes at the next step tomorrow with no penalty and no lost progress.
