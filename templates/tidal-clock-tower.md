# The Tidal Clock Tower

*A cooperative clue game about order, bells and tide steps*

**Category:** Games · **Ages:** Ages 7–11 · **Chinese name:** 潮汐钟塔 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

A cooperative clue game set in a harbor clock tower. Players are tide keepers: four colored gates must open in exactly one order, and the only way to find it is to read the clues together, test an order against every clue, then count the gate bells and walk the keeper's steps. There is no timer and no losing — a wrong order just fails a clue, and the group talks it through and tries again. Every level's solution is verified unique before it ships.

## Features

- Cooperative clue-solving — the group wins together, no timer, no elimination
- Every level has exactly one order that fits all the clues — verified before shipping
- Three linked rounds per level: find the order, count the bells, walk the steps
- Hints first — the worked solution is only shown after the group has tried

## Sample content

### Sample Level · Level 1: The Four Tide Gates (complete playable session, with verified solution)

THE TIDAL CLOCK TOWER — LEVEL 1: THE FOUR TIDE GATES (a complete playable session, about 20 minutes)

THE KEEPER'S JOB: High on the harbor wall stands a clock tower, and under it, four tide gates that hold back the sea. Every evening the tide keeper opens the four gates — but they must open in exactly one order, or the harbor floods one dock and strands another. Nobody tells the keeper the order. The keeper works it out from clues, the way you will now. There is no clock ticking and no way to lose: if an order breaks a clue, the group talks it through and tries another. You are the keepers. Begin at the dock.

THE FOUR GATES (learn these first — they never change):
Blue Gate — rings 2 bells, and moves the keeper +3 steps.
Amber Gate — rings 3 bells, and moves the keeper −1 step.
Coral Gate — rings 5 bells, and moves the keeper +4 steps.
Green Gate — rings 4 bells, and moves the keeper −2 steps.
The keeper starts at Step 0 — the dock, at the bottom of the tower stairs. Steps can go up and down as gates open, but a good keeper never drops below the dock.

THE FOUR CLUES (read them aloud together):
(1) The Blue Gate opens first.
(2) The Coral Gate opens immediately before the Green Gate.
(3) The Green Gate opens after the Amber Gate.
(4) The Amber Gate does not open last.

PART 1 — FIND THE ORDER (worked): Clue 1 fixes Blue first, so Blue takes place 1 and three places remain. Clue 2 says Coral and Green must sit side by side as a pair, Coral then Green — and with Blue already first, that pair can only sit in places 2–3 or places 3–4. Try places 2–3 first: then Amber would be left alone in place 4 — Amber would open last, but Clue 4 says Amber does not open last. Worse, Green (place 3) would come before Amber (place 4), and Clue 3 says Green opens after Amber. Two clues broken — the pair cannot sit in places 2–3. So the pair sits in places 3–4, and Amber takes the only place left, place 2. THE ORDER: Blue → Amber → Coral → Green. Now check all four clues against this order, one by one: (1) Blue opens first ✓. (2) Coral opens immediately before Green ✓ — they sit side by side in places 3 and 4. (3) Green opens after Amber ✓ — place 4 comes after place 2. (4) Amber does not open last ✓ — Amber is second, Green is last. All four clues pass — and because every other arrangement broke at least one clue, this is the only order that works.

PART 2 — COUNT THE BELLS (worked): As each gate opens, its bells ring out over the harbor. In the opening order the bells ring: Blue 2, Amber 3, Coral 5, Green 4. Keep a running total as you go: after Blue, 2. After Amber, 2 + 3 = 5. After Coral, 5 + 5 = 10. After Green, 10 + 4 = 14. Total bells: 14. Now the keeper's favourite question: the two middle bells of the evening are the 7th and 8th bells — at which gate do they ring? Count along the gates: Blue covers bells 1–2, Amber covers bells 3–5, Coral covers bells 6–10, Green covers bells 11–14. Bells 7 and 8 both fall inside Coral's stretch — so both middle bells ring at the Coral Gate.

PART 3 — WALK THE STEPS (worked): Last job: walk the tower stairs the way the gates move the keeper, starting at Step 0, the dock. Blue moves the keeper +3 → Step 3. Amber moves the keeper −1 → Step 2. Coral moves the keeper +4 → Step 6. Green moves the keeper −2 → Step 4. The keeper finishes at Step 4, near the top of the stairs — and look back down the walk: 0, 3, 2, 6, 4. The keeper never drops below the dock (Step 0). A perfect evening's work.

CLOSE THE LEVEL: Talk together about which clue did the most work — most keepers say Clue 2, because the Coral–Green pair only had two homes, and one of them broke two clues at once. Then replay with a fresh clue set: the gates stay the same, the bells and steps stay the same, but the order changes — and there is always exactly one order that fits every clue. That is the keeper's promise.
