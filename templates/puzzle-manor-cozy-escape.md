# Puzzle Manor: Cozy Escape Rooms

*Solve chained clues, unlock cozy rooms, escape by thinking — not rushing.*

**Category:** Games · **Ages:** Ages 7+ · **Chinese name:** 谜题庄园 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

Cozy escape rooms built on chained thinking: clues are collected on the Clue Chain Board, then ordered, combined, and sometimes backtracked — never solved by frantic tapping. A hint ladder always helps without giving the room away. There are no timers and no punishment; stars are earned only for chains completed, rooms finished with no hints, and hidden objects found. One or two players cooperate.

## Features

- Clue Chain Board: collect, order, combine, backtrack
- Multi-step chains — no single-tap solutions
- Hint ladder that helps without spoiling
- No timers, no punishment; stars for chains & no-hint clears
- 1–2 player co-op, per-adventure reset

## Sample content

### Room 1 · The Greenhouse Door (full worked playthrough)

Puzzle A — planting order: three seed packets show a Seed, a Drop, a Sun. The watering can's rhyme gives the order: Seed → Drop → Sun. Puzzle B — the kettle song: the greenhouse kettle whistles Long–Short–Long; match it on the chime rail to open the tool drawer. Puzzle C — the counting code: count what the room shows you — 4 leaves on the vine, 3 pots on the shelf, 2 birds at the window — code 4-3-2 opens the seed cabinet, where the three key pieces wait. The final lock: the door carving shows the planting symbols REVERSED — so combine the key pieces and enter Sun → Drop → Seed. The door opens onto the garden. The chain, start to finish: rhyme → order → song → count → combine → reverse. Every step used something an earlier step taught you.
