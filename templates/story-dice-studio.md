# Story Dice Studio

*Roll a hero, a place, and a problem — then tell a story that's never existed before.*

**Category:** Storytelling · **Ages:** Ages 6–12 · **Chinese name:** 故事骰子工作室 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

Nine dice — 3 Hero, 3 Place, 3 Problem, 300+ faces — start every story. The 5-step Storyteller's Map (Opening, Big Trouble, Clever Idea, Twist, Ending) gives the tale its shape, and the coach, Professor Plot the owl, only ever asks questions — the story stays the child's. Three creation modes: Tell It (voice), Write It, or Draw It (4-panel comic). Finished stories collect in the Studio Book, and a weekly Impossible Roll dares young authors to combine the strangest roll of all.

## Sample content

### Sample roll → Captain Bea and the Library That Flew Away

The roll: Hero — Captain Bea, a shy octopus inventor. Place — the Floating Library. Problem — the anchor rope has snapped.

The finished story: Captain Bea had invented many quiet things — a kettle that whispered, boots for puddles — but she had never invented anything brave. The morning the Floating Library's anchor rope snapped, the whole library began to drift toward the cloud-sea, with every book fluttering like a startled bird. Bea's eight arms shook. Then she remembered: a library is just ideas holding hands. She tied the longest bookmarks together, arm over arm over arm, and made a rope of stories — adventure to atlas, poem to pirate tale. The library held. That night Bea wrote a new label for her toolbox: “Bravery: inventing the thing you need, with hands still shaking.” Professor Plot's only comment: “What will Bea dare to invent next?”
