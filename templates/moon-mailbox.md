# The Moon Mailbox

*Write to the Moon tonight. Wake up to a story that answers you.*

**Category:** Storytelling · **Ages:** Ages 4–8 · **Chinese name:** 月亮信箱 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

Your child dictates or types one letter to the Moon at bedtime; the next morning (or next bedtime) a reply story arrives that truly answers that letter. The stories form a 12-episode serial season, The Season the Stars Went Dim, with a recurring cast: Luna the Moon Postkeeper, Pip the hedgehog mail-carrier, and the Comet Twins. Every letter and reply is kept in a keepsake Letter & Story Journal that parents can preview and print from the Parent Zone.

## Sample content

### Episode 1 · The Mitten on the Moonlight Train

Sam's letter (dictated at bedtime): “Dear Moon, I lost my red mitten at the park. It was my favorite. If you see it from up there, please tell it I miss it. Love, Sam.”

Episode 1 — The Mitten on the Moonlight Train. That night, Pip the hedgehog mail-carrier tucked Sam's letter into his satchel and climbed the moonbeam stairs. Luna the Moon Postkeeper read it twice, then looked down at the park with her silver telescope. “There,” she said. “Under the third bench, glowing a little, the way loved things do.” The Comet Twins hitched the Moonlight Train to a falling star, and Pip rode down with an empty seat saved beside him. He lifted the red mitten gently — it was cold, and a little lonely, and very glad to be found. All the way home the mitten rode by the window, watching the rooftops go by, and Pip told it, “Sam never stopped missing you, not for one minute.” In the morning the mitten was back on the porch rail, smelling faintly of starlight — and Sam knew the Moon had answered.
