# The Spice Blend Atelier

*Blend a small-batch spice mix by weight, taste, and note*

**Category:** Grown-ups · **Ages:** Adults · **Chinese name:** 香料调配工坊 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

A calm studio for grown-ups who want to make one small, repeatable spice blend instead of buying a jar they will never finish: pick a blend family, a heat level and the flavour accents you love; the atelier teaches the ratio in plain language, walks you through toasting, grinding and weighing by the gram, then has you taste, adjust one thing at a time, and write a label and tasting note you can actually repeat next time. Every blend is weighed so the total adds up exactly, sessions are short and private by default, and the sample blend below is fully worked and original.

## Features

- Weighed by the gram — every blend total adds up exactly
- Toast, grind, mix, rest: one calm step at a time
- Taste-and-adjust step: change one thing, note why
- A repeatable label + tasting note for every jar

## Sample content

### Sample Session · Bright Finishing Blend No. 1: Toasted Seed & Citrus (complete, weighed to 30 g)

HOW THIS SESSION WORKS: The atelier gives you a weighed ratio, you toast and grind once, then you taste and adjust ONE thing at a time and write down why.

THE BLEND — BRIGHT FINISHING BLEND No. 1 (Small jar, 30 g total)
Coriander seed, toasted and ground — 9 g
Cumin seed, toasted and ground — 6 g
Fennel seed, toasted and ground — 4 g
Fine sea salt — 4 g
Dried orange zest, finely ground — 3 g
Dried thyme leaves — 2 g
Golden sugar — 1 g
Mild chilli flakes — 1 g
Check the maths together: 9 + 6 + 4 + 4 + 3 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 30 g exactly. If you make a double jar, double every line — never just double the salt.

STEP 1 — TOAST (3 minutes): Put only the whole seeds (coriander, cumin, fennel) in a dry pan over medium heat. Shake the pan, do not walk away. When the coriander smells warm and citrusy — about 2 to 3 minutes — tip everything straight onto a cold plate. Hot seeds left in the pan keep cooking and turn bitter.

STEP 2 — COOL AND GRIND (6 minutes): Let the seeds cool fully, about 5 minutes; warm seeds grind into a paste. Grind to a coarse sand, not a fine powder — you want a little texture for a finishing blend. Stir in the salt, orange zest, thyme, sugar and chilli flakes.

STEP 3 — REST: Seal the jar and rest it overnight if you can. On day one the cumin shouts; by the next morning the orange and fennel have joined in. If you must taste now, taste again tomorrow before you adjust anything.

STEP 4 — TASTE THREE WAYS: (a) a pinch on warm flatbread brushed with olive oil; (b) a pinch on a cucumber slice with a drop of lemon; (c) a pinch on buttered corn or plain rice. Write one line for each: what you notice first, what lingers, and whether the salt feels right.

STEP 5 — ADJUST ONE THING ONLY: Too bitter? Next batch, cut the orange zest from 3 g to 2 g and add that gram to the coriander. Too flat? Add 1 g more fennel, taken from the cumin. Too salty for finishing? Drop the salt to 3 g and add 1 g coriander. Change one line, write the reason on the label — that note is what makes the next jar better instead of just different.

THE LABEL (copy this shape): Bright Finishing Blend No. 1 — made by Alex — 30 g — gentle warmth — best on flatbread, cucumber, corn and rice — store airtight, away from light, and use within 8 weeks for the brightest flavour.

YOUR TURN (the atelier prompt): Pick your family and heat level, weigh your first jar exactly as written, taste it three ways tomorrow, and adjust one single line. The atelier keeps the maths honest and never rushes you.
