# Echo Grove

*A listening memory game — hear the sequence, play it back*

**Category:** Games · **Ages:** Ages 5–10 · **Chinese name:** 回声林地 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

Echo Grove turns listening into a game. The grove plays a short sequence of gentle tones, leaf chimes and bird-like whistles — then it is your turn to play it back in order. Sequences grow one sound at a time, and every level adds a twist: a rhythm to copy, a sound that appears twice, or a quiet rest beat to wait through. There are no timers and no scores to chase — miss a sound and the grove simply plays it again more slowly, with the pattern shown in glowing leaves so every child can succeed by listening, looking, or both. Families can play solo, pass-and-play, or in duet mode where two players each own half the sounds.

## Features

- Listen-then-play-back sequences that grow one sound at a time
- No timers, no elimination — replays slow down and show glowing-leaf hints
- Solo, pass-and-play, and cooperative duet modes
- Gentle nature sounds only — no sudden loud notes
- Parent view: levels completed and longest sequence remembered

## What's included

## At a glance

- Hear it first — the grove plays leaf, chime, leaf, whistle, and the leaves glow as each sound plays
- Play it back — tap the same sounds in order; miss one and the grove replays it slower, never with a buzzer
- Duet mode — two players each own half the sounds and must listen to each other to finish the sequence together

## Sample content

### Sample · Grove One, Levels 1–3 (complete playable opening)

HOW TO PLAY (read once, then just listen): The grove plays sounds. You play the same sounds back in the same order. Sounds: LEAF (soft shaker, green leaf), CHIME (clear bell, gold ring), WHISTLE (bird-like two-note whistle, blue feather), DRUM (soft low tap, brown log — joins in Grove Two).

LEVEL 1 — THREE SOUNDS. Grove plays: LEAF, CHIME, LEAF. Correct playback: LEAF, CHIME, LEAF. Teaching note: the first and last sounds match — point that out after the child succeeds: 'The sequence starts and ends with the same sound. That pattern is called a sandwich pattern.'

LEVEL 2 — FOUR SOUNDS WITH A DOUBLE. Grove plays: WHISTLE, LEAF, LEAF, CHIME. Correct playback: WHISTLE, LEAF, LEAF, CHIME. If the child plays WHISTLE, LEAF, CHIME: the grove replays at half speed with leaves glowing, and says: 'Listen for the sound that happens twice in a row.' No penalty, unlimited replays.

LEVEL 3 — FIVE SOUNDS WITH A REST. Grove plays: CHIME, LEAF, REST (one silent beat, shown as an empty grey leaf), WHISTLE, CHIME. Correct playback: CHIME, LEAF, wait one beat, WHISTLE, CHIME. Teaching note: silence is part of the music — count 'one' silently on the rest.

SOLUTIONS VERIFIED: Level 1 = L-C-L (3), Level 2 = W-L-L-C (4, double L in positions 2–3), Level 3 = C-L-rest-W-C (5, rest in position 3). Sequences above are the exact answer keys.
