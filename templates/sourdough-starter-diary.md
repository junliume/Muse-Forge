# The Sourdough Starter Diary

*A calm kitchen journal for feeding, timing and trusting your starter*

**Category:** Grown-ups · **Ages:** Grown-ups · **Chinese name:** 天然酵种日记 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

A kitchen companion for home bakers who keep a sourdough starter: log each feeding with ratio and temperature, watch rise and fall without anxiety, plan a bake around your real schedule, and learn your starter's own rhythm over weeks. No streak pressure and no scolding — just clear records, gentle timing math done for you, and notes that turn hunches into knowledge you can reuse next bake.

## Features

- Feeding log with ratio, flour and room temperature
- Rise-window planner that works backwards from when you want to bake
- Baker's-percentage calculator with the math shown step by step
- Starter rhythm page: peak times by temperature, learned from your own logs
- Rescue notes for sluggish, hungry or over-acidic starters

## What's included

- Feeding log
- Rise-window planner
- Baker's-percentage calculator
- Starter rhythm page

## At a glance

- Feed, note the temperature, and mark the jar — the diary charts rise against time
- Plan backwards: pick your bake time and the planner lays out feed, mix, shape and bake
- Baker's percentages with every step shown — flour is always 100%, everything else follows

## How it works

1. Log each feeding with its ratio, flour blend and room temperature, and mark the jar level
2. Watch the diary chart rise against time and plan backwards from when you want to bake
3. Use the baker's-percentage calculator and the rhythm page to bake with your starter's own timing

- **Baking rhythm** (single)
  - Choices: Daily (counter, fed every day) / Weekly (fridge between bakes) / Twice a month (fridge, longer rests) / Just starting out

- **Usual feed ratio** (single)
  - Choices: 1:1:1 (fast, for a bake today) / 1:2:2 (gentle, all-purpose) / 1:3:3 (slow, warm kitchens) / 1:5:5 (very slow, long rests)
  - Default: 1:2:2 (gentle, all-purpose)
- **Starter flour blend** (single)
  - Choices: All bread flour / Half bread flour, half whole wheat / Mostly whole rye / Gluten-free blend
  - Default: Half bread flour, half whole wheat
- **Session length** (single)
  - Choices: About 10 min / About 20 min / About 40 min
  - Default: About 20 min
- **Reminder style** (single)
  - Choices: Gentle notes, no streaks / A simple daily nudge / Only when a bake is planned / No reminders
  - Default: Gentle notes, no streaks
- **Log focus** (multi)
  - Choices: Rise & peak times / Flavour notes / Bake results & crumb / Temperature patterns / Experiments & what-ifs

## Sample content

### Sample · Day One with Mabel the Starter: First Feed, First Rise, First Loaf Plan (complete worked day)

A complete worked day. 09:00 FEED — You keep 30 g of starter (name it Mabel if you like; any name works). Feed at a 1:2:2 ratio: 30 g starter + 60 g flour (30 g whole wheat + 30 g bread flour) + 60 g water at 26°C. Total in jar: 30 + 60 + 60 = 150 g. Stir, mark the level with a band, cover loosely. Kitchen is 22°C. 11:00 CHECK — Mabel is up about one-third, with small bubbles at the sides. Note: 'Slightly domed, smells milky, not yet tangy.' No action needed. 13:30 PEAK — Mabel has doubled to the band's double mark, dome is just flattening, smell is pleasantly tangy like green apple. From feed to peak at 22°C: 4.5 hours. Log it — this is your first rhythm data point: at 22°C, 1:2:2 peaks in about 4.5 hours. 14:00 BAKE PLAN (works backwards from a 19:00 bake tomorrow is NOT used here; this plan is for a same-day simple loaf): LEVAIN: use 100 g of the ripe starter now. DOUGH MATH with baker's percentages (flour = 100%): bread flour 500 g = 100%; water 375 g = 375/500 = 75%; ripe starter 100 g = 100/500 = 20%; salt 10 g = 10/500 = 2%. Total dough weight: 500 + 375 + 100 + 10 = 985 g. Check: percentages sum in weight terms only via flour base — never add percentages directly to get weight. MIX 14:00, rest 30 min, three gentle folds at 14:30, 15:00, 15:30, shape 17:30, proof, bake 19:00 at 230°C for 20 min covered + 20 min uncovered. EVENING NOTE: 'Crust sang as it cooled. Crumb open, flavour mild. Next time: feed the night before for more tang.' STARTER CARE: return 30 g to a clean jar, feed 1:2:2 again (30+60+60=150 g), refrigerate if you bake weekly, or leave out with a morning feed if you bake daily. END OF SAMPLE — every number above checks: ratios, totals and percentages are exact.
