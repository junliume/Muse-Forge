# Clue & Supper

*Host a murder-free mystery night: assign secret roles, serve the courses, and let your guests solve it between starter and dessert.*

**Category:** Grown-ups · **Ages:** Ages 18+ · **Chinese name:** 线索与晚餐 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

A dinner-party mystery hosting kit with no murder and no acting required. Three starter cases — The Vanishing Vermouth, The Chef's Missing Notebook, and The Last Train to Millbrook. The Host Control Room releases clues course by course; every guest gets a one-page Character Brief (public story, one private fact, one thing to hide); and the Sealed Solution carries the full deduction chain, so even the host can play along and still be surprised.

## Features

- 3 starter cases, murder-free, no acting required
- Host Control Room: clues released course by course
- One-page Guest Character Briefs: public story / private fact / one secret
- Sealed Solution with full deduction chain — the host can play too
- Printable kit or phone mode with room code; awards + debrief

## Sample content

### Case 1 · The Vanishing Vermouth — host opening + first private clue

Host opening script (read aloud as the starter lands): “Hartley House, autumn 1962. In the cellar sits a single bottle of Vermouth di Torino, promised to tonight's toast. An hour ago it stood in the locked cabinet. The cabinet is open. The bottle is gone. The key — the only key — was returned to its hook in the kitchen, by hands unknown. One of you knows more than your soup spoon should.” Seating-plan place-card clue (tucked under Mara Vane's plate): “Your card smells faintly of orange peel.” Mara Vane's first private clue (only she sees it): “At 7:25 you passed the kitchen and saw the cabinet key hanging on its hook — but you had heard it jingle in the cellar passage at 7:10. Someone returned it in between. Hide this until the fish course.” The Sealed Solution holds the full chain; the host never has to peek.
