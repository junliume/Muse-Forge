# Pattern Builders: Logic & Early Coding

*Sequences, loops, and debugging — coding thinking with no code to type.*

**Category:** Education · **Also listed under:** Games · **Ages:** Ages 5–8 · **Chinese name:** 模式建造师 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

Patterns, sequencing with instruction tiles, loops, and if/then thinking — plus a formal debugging method kids can recite: find the FIRST broken step, fix one thing, re-test. The robot Bolt follows instructions exactly as given, mistakes included, which makes every bug funny instead of frustrating. Answer-locking applies to check questions only; Build/Run missions are retryable by design, because debugging is the lesson — every run and fix is logged for parents.

## Sample content

### Mission 1 · Bolt and the Star

Pattern warm-up: 🔺 🔺 ⬜ 🔺 🔺 ⬜ 🔺 🔺 __ — what comes next? Answer: ⬜. Worked guidance, the chunking way: “Don't read one shape at a time — read chunks. The chunk is 🔺🔺⬜, and it repeats. After two triangles, the chunk says a square.” Sequence mission: guide Bolt to the star in 6 steps: Forward, Forward, Turn Right, Forward, Up the Ramp, Wave. Hint ladder: (1) “Watch Bolt's feet, not the star.” (2) “Bolt turned one step too late.” (3) “Move Turn Right one tile earlier.” Loop extension: the long bridge is Repeat 4 × [Forward, Up] — one loop tile instead of eight steps. Then: create-your-own puzzle and hand it to a parent to break.
