# Porch Garden Notebook

*A calm seasonal notebook for a small green corner*

**Category:** Grown-ups · **Ages:** Grown-ups · **Chinese name:** 阳台花园笔记本 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

A gentle planning and journaling studio for a tiny garden — a porch, windowsill, raised bed or small backyard corner. Each session helps you notice light and drainage first, choose a few plants that suit the season, plant them step by step, and keep a simple care calendar you can actually follow. No expertise assumed and no long shopping lists: you start with what you have, three plants at a time, and a notebook that remembers what you sowed and when.

## Features

- Light-and-drainage check before any planting
- Season-first plant picks (three at a time)
- Step-by-step planting session
- Simple weeks 1-4 care calendar
- Notebook pages for sowing dates, first shoots and notes

## Sample content

### Sample Session · Session One: The Herb Corner in Autumn (complete worked session)

OPENING NOTE: Autumn suits cool-season herbs, so this session plants exactly three — parsley, chives and cilantro (coriander leaf). One pot per herb, three pots in all.

STEP 1 — CHECK THE LIGHT. Put the pots where they will live and look at them morning, midday and late afternoon. The herbs in this session need bright light for most of the day. If the porch is shaded all day, move the pots to the brightest edge before planting — light first, plants second.

STEP 2 — CHECK DRAINAGE. Pour a cup of water into an empty pot: it should drain freely from the holes within a minute. If it pools, add holes or raise the pot on small feet. Never plant into standing water.

STEP 3 — FILL AND LEVEL. Fill each pot with general potting compost to 2 cm below the rim and level it gently. One pot per herb, so each label stays true.

STEP 4 — PLANT. Chives go in as a small clump, pressed to the same depth they grew at. Parsley seedlings go in 15 cm apart, because the leaves spread. Cilantro is sown directly as seed, 1 cm deep, because it dislikes being moved — write that reason on its notebook page.

STEP 5 — WATER IN. Water slowly until it just runs from the base, then stop. The surface should be evenly damp, not flooded.

STEP 6 — LABEL. Write the herb name and today's sowing or planting date on each label, and copy the same three lines into the notebook: Parsley — planted today. Chives — planted today. Cilantro — sown today, seed 1 cm deep.

STEP 7 — CARE CALENDAR, WEEKS 1–4. Week 1: keep the surface just damp; check every other day. Week 2: look for cilantro shoots, and thin crowded seedlings to 10 cm apart. Week 3: water at the base in the morning, and check the undersides of leaves when you water. Week 4: snip the first chive tips and a few parsley stems from the outside of the plant, and note the date and the taste in the notebook.

CLOSING LINE: Three pots, three labels, one page — the garden has begun, and the notebook knows when everything happened.
