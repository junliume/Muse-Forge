# Poetry Forms Studio

*Learn a classic form, draft it line by line, revise with care*

**Category:** Grown-ups · **Ages:** Adults · **Chinese name:** 诗歌形式工坊 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

A studio for grown-ups who want the pleasure of a real poetic form without homework dread: pick a form (haiku sequence, tanka, sonnet starter, villanelle starter, or free verse with one constraint), a theme, and how much craft guidance you want; the studio teaches the form in two minutes, walks you line by line with an optional syllable counter on every line, then helps you revise one weak line together before you save or print a keepsake card. Sessions are short, private by default, and every sample poem in the studio is original and syllable-counted by hand.

## Features

- Five classic forms, each taught in two minutes
- Optional syllable count shown on every line
- Revise-together step: fix one weak line, never the whole poem at once
- Private by default — share aloud or print a keepsake card only when you choose

## What's included

## Sample content

### Sample Session · Haiku Sequence: Market Morning After Rain (complete, syllable-counted)

HOW THIS SESSION WORKS: The studio states the form (haiku: three lines of 5, 7 and 5 syllables), shows each line with its count, then revises one weak line together at the end.

HAIKU 1 — THE AWNING
Rain on the awning (5)
drums a soft code over plums (7)
the seller laughs once (5)

HAIKU 2 — THE ROAD HOME
Mist thins on the road (5)
a bicycle bell rings twice (7)
bread steam in cold air (5)

HAIKU 3 — PUDDLES
Puddles hold the sky (5)
boots step around bright mirrors (7)
home smells of ginger (5)

REVISE TOGETHER — one weak line, fixed: First draft: "Rain is falling on the market awning" (10 syllables — too long for an opening haiku line, and it tells instead of shows). The studio asks: what is the single picture? Revised line: "Rain on the awning" (5) — the picture survives, the padding goes.

BONUS FORM PREVIEW — TANKA (5-7-5-7-7), same morning:
After the rain stops (5)
the lane shines with fallen leaves (7)
we walk more slowly (5)
your sleeve brushes my sleeve once (7)
and neither of us steps back (7)

YOUR TURN (the studio prompt): Pick one small picture from your own morning — a sound, a colour, one small gesture — and give it three lines: 5, 7, 5. The studio counts with you and never grades you.
