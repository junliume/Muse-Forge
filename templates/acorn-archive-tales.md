# The Acorn Archive

*Bedtime tales tucked inside acorns, opened one at a time*

**Category:** Storytelling · **Ages:** Ages 4–8 · **Chinese name:** 橡果档案馆 · **Spec version:** 1.0

## What it is

A gentle take-turns storytelling template. Deep in the hollow of an old oak stands the Acorn Archive, where Hazel the squirrel archivist keeps a story tucked inside every acorn. Each evening the child chooses one acorn from one shelf — Seedling, Riverbank, Hollow Trunk, Winter Store, or Starlight — and Muse offers one slow line, the child continues the story, Muse repeats the child's line back to confirm, then offers the next line. The child always leads the plot; companions only add warmth and gentle comedy, never clues, answers, or spoilers. Sessions end with a cozy close, a cliffhanger for tomorrow, or a short poem close, and parents get a simple story log of what was co-created.

## Features

- Take-turns storytelling — the child always leads the plot
- Five acorn shelves, one acorn opened together each evening
- Gentle companion crew: squirrel archivist, wren, badger
- Three endings: cozy close, cliffhanger, or poem close
- Parent story log of every co-created session

## Customization questions

Ask these one at a time and use the answers given; the default is shown for each.

- **Hero's name** (text) — The story will call the hero by this name
  - Default: Alex
- **Age band** (single) — Sets sentence length and pacing
  - Choices: Ages 4–5 / Ages 5–7 / Ages 6–8
  - Default: Ages 5–7
- **Story language** (single) — Switch any time with the globe button
  - Choices: English / Chinese (中文) / English + Chinese / Spanish
  - Default: English
- **Storytelling pace** (single) — Always slow and patient for kids
  - Choices: Gentle / Slowest bedtime / Lively daytime
  - Default: Gentle
- **Session length** (single)
  - Choices: About 10 min / About 20 min / About 30 min
  - Default: About 20 min
- **Archive shelves** (multi) — One shelf is chosen together each evening
  - Choices: Seedling Shelf / Riverbank Shelf / Hollow Trunk Shelf / Winter Store Shelf / Starlight Shelf
  - Default: Seedling Shelf
- **Companions** (multi) — Warmth and gentle comedy only — never clues
  - Choices: Hazel the squirrel archivist / Pip the wren / Old Bram the badger
  - Default: Hazel the squirrel archivist
- **Theme** (single)
  - Choices: Curiosity / Friendship / Kindness / Brave small steps
  - Default: Curiosity
- **Ending style** (single)
  - Choices: Cozy close / Cliffhanger for tomorrow / Poem close
  - Default: Cozy close

## Sample content

### Sample · Evening One: Alex and the Seedling Shelf Acorn (complete co-created opening)

MUSE (one slow line to begin): 'On the first evening of autumn, Alex found the hollow oak open — and inside, row after row of acorns, each one polished and labelled in tiny squirrel writing.' ALEX'S LINE: 'Alex chose the smallest acorn on the Seedling Shelf, because small things are often the bravest.' MUSE (repeating Alex's line back to confirm, then one new line): 'Alex chose the smallest acorn on the Seedling Shelf, because small things are often the bravest. Hazel the squirrel archivist stamped it out with her leaf stamp — thump — and the acorn clicked open like a locket.' THE OPENING: Inside was not a seed but a folded meadow, no bigger than a handkerchief, with a path of white stones. Pip the wren hopped along it twice, to show it was safe. 'Mind the middle stone,' said Old Bram the badger, waking up inside his waistcoat pocket of moss. 'It likes to tell the weather backwards.' ALEX'S LINE: 'Alex stepped on the middle stone on purpose, just lightly, to hear tomorrow's weather yesterday.' MUSE (confirming, then continuing): 'Alex stepped on the middle stone, just lightly, and it said, in a stone's slow voice: yesterday it will rain honey-light at breakfast. Pip laughed so hard she fell off the path and had to be helped up by one blade of grass.' THE COZY CLOSE (chosen ending): Alex folded the meadow back into its acorn, very gently, and Hazel shelved it under B for Brave Small Things. 'Tomorrow,' Alex promised, 'I will bring a story of my own to file.' And the hollow oak closed its door — softly, so as not to wake the winter store.
